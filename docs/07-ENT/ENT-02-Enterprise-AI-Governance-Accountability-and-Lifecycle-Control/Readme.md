# ENT-02 — Enterprise AI Governance, Accountability and Lifecycle Control

## Purpose

ENT-02 explains how enterprise AI governance is **assigned, organized, operated, and maintained across the lifecycle of AI systems**.

The chapter focuses on:

- Accountability
- Decision rights
- Governance responsibilities
- Governance operating models
- Governance mechanisms
- Approval and escalation
- Governance evidence
- Lifecycle governance
- Material change control
- Governance reassessment

The central question is:

> **Who is accountable for enterprise AI decisions, how are decision rights and responsibilities structured, and how does governance operate throughout the AI lifecycle?**

ENT-02 is specifically concerned with governance of enterprise AI systems. It is not a generic corporate governance, compliance, policy, risk-management, security, or organizational-design chapter.

---

## Learning Path

The chapter follows a deliberate **WHO → HOW → WHEN** progression:

```text
WHO?
Accountability + Decision Rights
        │
        ▼
HOW?
Governance Operating Models + Control Mechanisms
        │
        ▼
WHEN?
Lifecycle Governance + Material Change Control
```

This progression moves from identifying **who has authority and accountability**, to understanding **how governance becomes operational**, and finally to understanding **how governance remains active as AI systems evolve**.

Architecture and governance interact, but they answer different engineering questions.

ENT-02 may establish accountability and governance around architecture, risk, security, data, providers, economics, resilience, and production decisions without re-teaching the substantive disciplines owned by those areas.

## Governance Focus

The chapter should be understood as a progression:

```text
Accountability
      ↓
Decision Rights
      ↓
Governance Responsibilities
      ↓
Governance Operating Model
      ↓
Governance Mechanisms
      ↓
Approval + Escalation
      ↓
Evidence + Review
      ↓
Lifecycle Governance
      ↓
Material Change
      ↓
Reassessment
```

The purpose is not to memorize this sequence as a mandatory enterprise process.

It provides a mental model for understanding how governance connects **authority, accountability, decisions, controls, evidence, and lifecycle review**.

## Recommended Study Sequence

Read the files in order:

```text
01 — Accountability and Decision Rights
             ↓
02 — Governance Operating Models and Control Mechanisms
             ↓
03 — Lifecycle Governance and Material Change Control
```

The sequence is intentional:

1. Establish **who** is accountable and who has decision rights.
2. Establish **how** governance is organized and operated.
3. Establish **when** governance applies, must be reviewed, and may need reassessment.

The three files should therefore be studied as a connected progression rather than as independent governance topics.

---

## Completion Guidance

ENT-02 is complete when the learner can reason about an enterprise AI system and determine:

- Who is accountable for important AI decisions and outcomes?
- Who has the authority to approve, reject, or escalate those decisions?
- How are responsibilities distributed between central and domain teams?
- What mechanisms make governance operational?
- What evidence demonstrates that governance decisions occurred?
- How does governance continue after deployment?
- What changes may require governance reassessment?
- How should materiality influence the depth of renewed review?

The goal is to develop the ability to reason about **accountability, authority, governance mechanisms, and lifecycle control in real enterprise AI systems**.
